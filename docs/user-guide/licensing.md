## What is free

An individual may use, copy and modify the AgenticOrg platform without a fee and without
asking, for their own personal purposes, as long as they are not acting for, on behalf
of, or for the benefit of any organisation. Personal study, trying it out on your own
computer and hobby projects are examples.
